<Note status="Research direction">
**Nothing on this page exists.** There is no backtesting engine, no strategy
discovery machinery, no validation harness, and no product UI. These are the
questions the infrastructure is being built to make answerable — written here
as direction, never as feature.
</Note>

AlphaLens is described publicly as quantitative trading research. That phrase
names the destination. The live data layer is the part that has to exist first,
and it is the part that is built.

## Why the order matters

A backtester over a broken or partial feed produces confident nonsense. Every
decisions so far — the strict minute grid, the atomic per-minute transaction,
the canonical `ts_utc`, the never-fail-closed posture, the pinned view shape —
is a prerequisite for research results anyone could reproduce. The
infrastructure is not a detour on the way to backtesting; it is what would make
a backtest mean anything.

## The three directions

- **[Backtesting](/docs/research-directions/backtesting)** — replay history
  through a strategy and measure it honestly.
- **[Discovery](/docs/research-directions/discovery)** — search the space of
  candidate strategies without fooling yourself.
- **[Validation](/docs/research-directions/validation)** — decide whether a
  survivor deserves capital.

Each page states what the direction needs, and what AlphaLens already has or
does not have toward it.

## What this site will not do

It will not publish backtest results, performance figures, or strategy
recommendations, and it will not describe a backtesting or discovery engine as
though one were shipped. When these directions turn into running software, the
labels on this page change — and not before.
