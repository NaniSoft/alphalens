title: Backtesting

<Note status="Research direction">No backtesting engine exists in
AlphaLens.</Note>

Backtesting means replaying historical market state through a strategy's rules
and measuring the result. For an options-and-index universe, the interesting
version needs **the full chain at each point in time** — not closes, and not a
single expiry — because the strategy's inputs and its achievable fills both
depend on the whole surface.

## What AlphaLens already has toward it

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|
| Full NIFTY + BANKNIFTY chain, every strike, per minute | **live** since 2026-08-26 |
| IV and Greeks attached per leg, no local re-pricing | **live** |
| One-minute OHLCV for spot, VIX, futures and every captured leg | **live**, post-close backfill |
| A timestamp an analyst can range over across sources | **approved** — the contract's `ts_utc` |
| EOD summaries (PCR, max-OI strikes, OHLC) | **live** |

The per-minute snapshot is the asset. It is append-only, transaction-atomic,
and keyed on a strict minute grid, which is what a replay needs to reconstruct
the same market state twice.

## What is missing

- **History depth.** The collector went live in August 2026; the archive grows
  one trading day at a time. There is no vendor history behind it.
- **Expired F&O contract history.** Fyers does not serve expired contracts from
  `/data/history`; the official answer is a paid third-party vendor. This is
  the **hard** gap for long-horizon option backtesting, and it is not solved.
- **Higher-timeframe candles.** Stored 1-minute rows can be aggregated on read,
  but no native 5m/15m/1h/daily series is persisted.
- **Execution modelling.** Nothing in the platform models fills, slippage, or
  impact. Captured bid/ask is a start, not an answer.
- **A replay engine.** There is none.

## The honest summary

The data a backtester would consume is being captured today, and its shape was
chosen with replay in mind. The backtester itself is future work, and its
results would carry a start-date caveat for years.
