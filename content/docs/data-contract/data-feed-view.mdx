The view is specified and its column list is pinned by a contract-stability

</Note>

## One row shape for nine kinds of fact

`data_feed_view` is a `UNION ALL` across the Fyers tables and the six external
tables. Every row carries the same columns; a source that has nothing to say in
a column writes `NULL` there.

```
ts_utc · symbol · category · source · underlying · instrument_type
expiry_epoch · ltp · bid · ask · volume · oi
report_date · headline · message · indicator_name · action_type
```

A Fyers quote row fills the price columns and leaves `headline` and
`indicator_name` null. A news row fills `headline` and leaves `ltp` and `oi`
null. An agent filtering `WHERE category = 'news'` and one reading raw price
action are reading the same shape.

## Why a view and not tables-for-agents

Three reasons, all from the approved design:

1. **The read path is live-only.** No daily snapshot tables for agents to
   reason about; the view reads what is there.
2. **Shape stability is the contract.** Prompts are written against the column
   list. The test that pins it is what makes "contract" mean something.
3. **Categories, not tables.** An analyst asks for a *category* of fact at a
   *minute*; the view is that query, already normalised.

## An example slice

One minute, one symbol, every source that has anything to say about it:

```sql
SELECT category, source, ltp, oi, headline, indicator_name
FROM data_feed_view
WHERE symbol = 'NSE:NIFTY'
  AND ts_utc BETWEEN '2026-09-19T03:45:00Z' AND '2026-09-19T03:46:00Z';
```

A Fyers quote at 09:15 IST and a news item published at 09:15 IST both resolve
to `03:45Z` and land in the same slice. That is the whole point of the
canonical timestamp — see
[Canonical timestamps & symbols](/docs/data-contract/timestamps-and-symbols).

## Where a category is silent

An analyst querying a category with no rows at a given minute gets an empty
result, not an error and not a fabricated value. The documented reading is
**"category silent this minute."** The existing `NO_DATA_AVAILABLE:` /
`DATA_UNAVAILABLE:` sentinels and their fallback chains are unchanged by the
contract — see [The never-fail-closed posture](/docs/data-contract/error-posture).
