Fyers has no endpoint for any of the six categories below — verified against
the Fyers-authored API reference, the live API docs, and the Fyers support KB.
These are structural gaps, not missing integration work, so they are filled
from outside.

## The picks

| Category | Source | Fallback | Cost | Nifty-500 coverage | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fundamentals & financials | NSE Python libraries (`nselib`, `nse-xbrl`) | yfinance `.NS` | Free, no key | High (exchange XBRL) | weekly full-scan |
| News | Economic Times RSS | MoneyControl RSS | Free | High | 30 min (plus yfinance news at 5 min) |
| Social sentiment | StockTwits public stream | Reddit (OAuth) | Free | Medium (large caps) | 15 min, watchlist only |
| Indian macro | RBI DBIE + eSankhyiki (MoSPI) | Trading Economics | Free primary | Exhaustive | weekly poll |
| Insider / bulk / block deals | NSE official CSV | TIGZIG Vigil | Free | High | daily ~21:00 post-close |
| Corporate actions | NSE official corporate-actions CSV | BSE official CSV | Free | High | daily ~21:00 post-close |
| Prediction markets | **None** | Polymarket, global only | — | **None** | — |

## Why those picks

**Fundamentals** come from NSE's own filings, parsed with XBRL for integrated
statements — the authoritative source, free, and aligned with the collector's
existing Python/Windows shape. Alpha Vantage was rejected here on coverage: it
accepts international symbols, but NSE coverage is sparse or absent.

**News** comes from Economic Times RSS because it is an official Indian-market
feed with dedicated markets and stocks endpoints, and it needs no key.
yfinance news was rejected as primary — it has been unreliable for NSE symbols.

**Sentiment** is the narrowest pick and is scoped deliberately: StockTwits'
public stream carries native bullish/bearish tags and no key, but Indian
coverage is effectively large-cap only via the `$SYM.NSE` form. It is polled on
a watchlist, not the whole universe.

**Macro** is split between the RBI's Database on Indian Economy (exhaustive,
manual CSV) and eSankhyiki for programmatic access to 500+ statistical
indicators. FRED — the source TradingAgents uses natively — carries no Indian
macro series at all.

**Prediction markets** are an accepted zero: Polymarket has no India-specific
contracts. Nothing is substituted, because nothing exists to substitute.

## Refresh cadence, and why it is not part of the contract

Cadence schedules the collectors; it is not a property of the contract itself.
That separation is what lets a poll frequency change — sentiment 15 minutes to
10, news 30 to 20 — without touching the shape an agent reads.

| Source | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Fyers quotes / options / candles | per-minute, live session |
| Fundamentals | weekly full-scan, per-symbol on re-pull |
| News | ET RSS 30 min; yfinance news 5 min |
| Sentiment | StockTwits 15 min, watchlist |
| Macro | weekly poll |
| Insider / corporate actions | daily ~21:00 |
| Constituents | weekly + rebalance diff |

## The compliance line

NSE's terms prohibit automated scraping and redistribution of exchange data
without written permission. The design therefore prefers the **official CSV
download** route for insider deals and corporate actions, and does not
re-package exchange data into a commercial feed.
