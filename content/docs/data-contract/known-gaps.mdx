## Streams Fyers exposes that AlphaLens does not yet pull

| Stream | Endpoint | Why it matters | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| L2 five-level order book for NIFTY/BANKNIFTY futures | `/data/depth` | order-book imbalance, execution quality, depth beyond top-of-book | unpulled — only USDINR, EURINR and MIDCPNIFTY futures are depth-polled today |
| Per-symbol futures OI and OI time series | `/data/depth` | futures positioning, roll analysis, build-up/unwind | unpulled — the quotes batch carries no OI for futures |
| Higher-timeframe candles (5m / 15m / 1h / daily) | `/data/history` | swing context, volatility regimes, any question above one minute | unpulled — 1m only; aggregate on read today |
| Sectoral index futures (FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY) | `/data/quotes`, `/data/depth` | sector rotation, hedging | MIDCPNIFTY captured; FINNIFTY unpulled |
| Tick-level trade tape | WebSocket `SymbolUpdate` | microstructure, tape reading | unpulled — no REST equivalent, needs a WebSocket client |
| Realized-volatility series | derived from `/data/history` | volatility regime detection | not persisted today |
| Intraday PCR and IV term structure | derived from the option chain | sentiment gauge, volatility surface, skew | per-leg IV is captured; the derived series is not persisted |

## Streams no Fyers endpoint can provide

These are structural — verified against the Fyers-authored API reference and
the support KB, not merely unplanned:

1. Corporate actions — dividends, splits, bonuses, rights, mergers.
2. Fundamentals — P/E, P/B, EPS, ROE, debt, book value, any valuation ratio.
3. Financial statements — balance sheet, cash flow, income statement.
4. News.
5. Insider transactions, bulk deals, block deals.
6. FII/DII flows.
7. Indian macro indicators — RBI rates, CPI, GDP, IIP, forex reserves.
8. Social sentiment.
9. Prediction markets.
10. Index composition change events.
11. The Nifty 500 constituent list (an NSE CSV, not a Fyers endpoint).

Categories 1–5 and 7–9 are exactly what the
[unified data contract](/docs/data-contract/index) fills from external sources.

## Gaps accepted, not filled

| Gap | Why it is accepted |
|---|---|
| Tick-level trade tape | No REST endpoint exists; a WebSocket client is a new transport layer, out of the minimum viable build. |
| Expired F&O contract history | Fyers does not serve expired contracts from `/data/history`; the official answer is a paid third-party vendor. A **hard** gap for long-horizon option backtesting. |
| FII/DII flows | No Fyers source; the NSE daily reports are the substitute. |
| Sentiment for small and mid caps | StockTwits' Indian coverage is large-cap; filling it needs paid vendors. |
| Prediction markets | Polymarket has no India-specific contracts. Nothing to substitute. |

## Two caveats that are easy to get wrong

**Futures OI is not in the quotes batch.** Fyers `/data/quotes` returns no
`oi` key for futures — confirmed by raw response inspection. Every other
futures field populates. Reading a missing futures OI as zero would be a bug,
not a simplification.

**Index volume is synthetic and recent.** Index volume is available only from
01 July 2025, and only for seven F&O-enabled indices, as the sum of traded
volume across current constituents — not exchange volume for the index itself.
