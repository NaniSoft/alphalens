The Python collector is the first implementation. The .NET port is the second —
not a rewrite of the data model, but a re-implementation of the same one, so
that the two are interchangeable against a single history.

## Why port at all

The decision, approved 2026-09-15, was to move to **.NET 10** (ASP.NET Core,
hosted services) with **no further Python feature work**. Python becomes a cold
standby after cutover. One Fyers app means one quota pool, so after cutover
exactly one capturer runs.

The port is two applications — **collector** (capture, EOD, candles, backup)
and **token-refresh** (the click-flow callback service). The ~250 lines the two
share are deliberately duplicated rather than extracted into a NuGet package.

## Schema-identical storage

Same per-day `snapshots-YYYY-MM-DD.db` files, same `summary.db` DDL including
the `candles_runs` table, same xz archive layout. History and Google Drive
continuity are preserved across the port: a day written by Python and a day
written by .NET are the same shape to any reader.

The rate limiter is a direct port too — the exact sliding windows (8/second
pacing plus the strict 190/minute cap), because the whole design depends on
staying 429-free. Breaching the per-minute cap more than three times in a day
blocks the Fyers account for the rest of that day.

## The NIFTY 500 quote universe

The feature that justified the port arrives with it, behind a config switch
(`quote_universe.enabled`):

- **Cash level-1 quotes for all 500 constituents**, the universe taken from
  the official NSE `ind_nifty500list.csv` with auto-refresh.
- **The two nearest monthly futures** for members present in the F&O
  instrument master.
- **No option chains** for the extended universe — chains stay on the two
  indices.

Budget: roughly **140 calls/min worst case against the 190 cap**, and about
**77k calls/day against the 100k cap**, with roughly **+2.5% storage**.

## The macro universe

An amendment approved 2026-09-16 widened the captured universe inside the same
session window, verified live against Fyers that morning:

| Segment | Verdict |
|---|---|
| NSE commodity futures (GOLD, SILVER, CRUDEOIL, NATURALGAS) | Quotes (OHLCV + VWAP) captured. **OI is not available** — `quotes` returns none, depth always reports `oi=0`, history returns `no_data`. A Fyers feed gap; depth polling excluded. |
| NSE currency futures (USDINR, EURINR) | Quotes, 480 bars/day of history, and **live depth OI** — verified. |
| Sectoral index futures | **Do not exist on NSE.** Cash-market index spots are captured instead: NIFTY IT / AUTO / PHARMA / METAL / FMCG / REALTY / MIDCPNIFTY / SMLCAP100, all verified live. |
| MIDCPNIFTY futures | Current + two months, candles, and live depth OI — verified. |

Three symbol masters are cached on the 12-hour rule: F&O (77 MB), CDS (11.5 MB)
and commodities (27 MB).

## What is deliberately not ported

**No healing logic.** The collector pulls today only — no lookback window, no
probe gate, no archive resurrection, no pending-resume. Data-integrity repair
belongs to a separate service; the collector captures the day and archives it.
The `candles_runs` markers consequently become pure status records.

The legacy headless-login module is not ported at all.
