## The per-minute snapshot

Each minute of the 09:15–15:30 IST session produces one snapshot. It is one
transaction, written atomically — a partial minute rolls back and is never
half-written.

| Instrument | Source call | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| Option chain (CE/PE, all selected strikes × selected expiries) | `/data/options-chain-v3` (`greeks=1`) | ltp, ltpch, ltpchp, bid, ask, **oi, oich, oichp, prev_oi, volume**, **iv, delta, gamma, theta, vega** |
| Index futures (current + next month) | `/data/quotes` (batched) | ltp, bid, ask, volume, prev_close, open, high, low, ch, chp, spread, atp |
| Index spot | `/data/quotes` | ltp, OHLC, ch, chp |
| India VIX | `/data/quotes` | ltp, OHLC, ch, chp |
| Snapshot health | (local) | n_legs, fetch_ms, errors |

## Selected expiries

Expiry selection is config-driven, not hard-coded:

- **NIFTY (7):** the nearest two weeklies, three monthlies, and the
  current-year and next-year December contracts.
- **BANKNIFTY (4):** three monthlies plus current-year December — no weeklies,
  per the SEBI rule.
- **Futures:** current-month and next-month per underlying.

The option-chain endpoint returns strikes around ATM rather than requiring an
enumerated list, so the chain scope is a single `strikecount` knob.

## The other capture jobs

| Job | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-market global cues | 08:45 and 10:00 IST | yfinance snapshots of the overnight board: ^GSPC, ^IXIC, ^DJI, ^VIX, CL=F, GC=F, USDINR=X, DX-Y.NYB, ^HSI, ^N225. |
| EOD daily summary | 15:31 IST | Computed locally from the day's captured rows: spot/VIX OHLC, PCR, max-OI strikes, total CE/PE OI. |
| Post-close candle backfill | 17:00 IST | True 1-minute OHLCV from `/data/history` for every symbol captured that day, written into that day's database. |

## What the snapshot deliberately is not

- **Candles are trade-based.** A leg that did not trade in a minute has no
  candle for that minute. Treat gaps as unknown, not zero. The per-minute
  snapshots, by contrast, are the complete every-leg grid.
- **No derived signals per minute.** PCR and max-OI are computed once at EOD,
  not on the tick. There is no local Black-Scholes — the chain arrives with
  IV and Greeks already attached.
- **No futures OI from the quotes batch.** Fyers `/data/quotes` does not
  return open interest for futures. Futures OI requires the `/data/depth`
  endpoint and is a documented extension, not a silent absence — see
  [Known gaps](/docs/data-contract/known-gaps).

## What it costs

A full snapshot cycle is **11 option-chain calls + 1 batched quotes call**,
about **2.5 seconds**. That is **12 calls/min against a 200/min Fyers cap** —
roughly 16× headroom. The live feasibility spike measured ~202 legs per index
per expiry at the configured chain scope, and a first production minute of
1,839 option legs plus 7 quote rows.
