AlphaLens is Nanisoft's quantitative research platform for the Indian (NSE/BSE)
market. It exists to put agents in front of a market they can actually read —
one coherent, timestamped, research-ready feed instead of a pile of vendor
exports.

Today that means one thing above all: **the live data layer**. A production
collector has captured the full NIFTY and BANKNIFTY option chain every market
minute since 2026-08-26, and has run on Kubernetes since 2026-09-08. An
approved data contract defines how that capture merges with the sources Fyers
structurally cannot provide. A multi-agent research pipeline is designed on top
of both and is not yet built.

<Note status="Status">
In active development. Everything on this site is labelled by one of three
tiers — **live**, **designed, not built**, or **research direction** — and the
labels are load-bearing. Read them.
</Note>

## The three tiers

| Tier | Meaning | What sits there |
|---|---|---|
| **Live** | Running in production now, stated in the present tense. | The market-data collector, its storage and archives, its operations, the .NET 10 port Phase 1. |
| **Approved / designed** | Approved and specified, not yet running. Always labelled. | The unified data contract (`data_feed_view`); the TradingAgents multi-agent wiring; the k3s VPS endgame. |
| **Research direction** | The trajectory the infrastructure points toward. Never presented as a feature. | Strategy backtesting, strategy discovery, strategy validation. |

This is deliberate. AlphaLens is not a trading platform, and it does not ship a
backtester, a strategy generator, or a validation harness today. Claiming any
of those would make the rest of the site untrustworthy — and the interesting
engineering here is real enough not to need inflation.

No strategy advice. No signals. No backtest results. No performance claims.
AlphaLens is market-data infrastructure and a research design; the site
documents both, and nothing else.
