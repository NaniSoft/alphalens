## The question

Capturing a **full** option chain, for both index underlyings, **every market
minute**, looked expensive. The assumption going in was that per-minute full
chain capture would need a streaming connection, careful strike enumeration,
and a rate budget with no room in it.

## What the research found

Reading the Fyers v3 API surface before writing any code turned the assumption
inside out:

- **REST `/data/options-chain-v3` delivers the whole per-minute snapshot per
  index in about two calls** — per-leg OI, OI change, IV, Greeks, LTP, bid/ask
  and volume, plus aggregate call/put OI, the expiry list, and India VIX inline.
- **The WebSocket carries no OI, IV or Greeks.** The transport that looked
  mandatory is the one that cannot serve the fields that matter. It stays
  pluggable behind a `data_provider` interface for later tick data, and is
  unused.
- **The rate cap is generous at this scale.** The per-minute budget fits inside
  a fraction of the 200/minute REST limit.

## What the live test measured

Ran against the live market on **2026-08-26**:

| Measurement | Result |
|---|---|
| ATM legs | real OI, IV and Greeks — not placeholders |
| Chain scope at the configured `strikecount` | ~202 legs per index per expiry |
| Full cycle | 12 calls in **3.46 s**, against a 60-second budget |
| Rate headroom | 12 calls/min against a 200/min cap — **~16×** |
| Response shape | option chain under `body["data"]`, quotes under `body["d"]`, Greeks under `leg["greeks"]` |

## What the spike decided

Three decisions settled in the spike and never reopened:

1. **REST-only transport.** The WebSocket is optional, not foundational.
2. **`strikecount` as the chain-scope knob** — the endpoint returns strikes
   around ATM, so no strike enumeration is needed at all.
3. **Capture raw fields, derive nothing per minute.** IV and Greeks arrive
   attached; PCR and max-OI are computed once at EOD. No local Black-Scholes.

## What it also found, and was recorded

Two empirical wrinkles surfaced during the build, both the kind that would
otherwise be rediscovered as bugs:

- **Futures OI is absent from `/data/quotes`** — no `oi` key at all for
  futures, confirmed by raw inspection. Every other futures field populates.
  Futures OI would need `/data/depth`, and is documented as an extension.
- **India VIX is richer from the quotes batch than from the chain's inline
  block.** The quotes-batch VIX carries full OHLC and volume; the inline block
  carries only LTP and change. The quotes batch is primary, the inline block a
  fallback.
